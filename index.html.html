About Our Business

Welcome to Sarvdany Fruits Supplier (सर्वज्ञ फ्रूट्स सप्लायर). Operating since 2005, we supply wholesale farm-fresh grapes with specialized cold-chain transport. Headquartered right at the major agricultural highway hub of Nandur Naka, Nashik, we connect local vineyards directly to markets all over India.
